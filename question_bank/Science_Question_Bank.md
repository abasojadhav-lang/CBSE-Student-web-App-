# Science Question Bank - Class 10th (Maharashtra Board)

## Science and Technology Part 1

### Important Topics
- **Gravitation**: Kepler's Laws, Acceleration due to gravity ($g$).
- **Periodic Classification**: Modern Periodic Table trends, Valency.
- **Chemical Reactions**: Types of reactions, Balancing equations, Oxidation & Reduction.
- **Electric Current**: Heating effect, Electric Motor/Generator (Diagrams).
- **Refraction of Light**: Laws of refraction, Dispersion.
- **Space Missions**: Artificial Satellites, Escape Velocity.

### Expected Questions

#### 1 Mark Questions (Objective)
1. Find the odd one out: Voltmeter, Ammeter, Galvanometer, Thermometre.
2. State True or False: The value of $g$ is maximum at the poles.
3. Write the IUPAC name of: $CH_3-CH_2-COOH$.
4. Fill in the blank: The refractive index of diamond is \_\_\_.
5. Write the correlation: Bronze : Alloy :: \_\_\_ : Amalgam.

#### 2 Marks Questions
1. Distinguish between Mass and Weight.
2. Draw a neat labeled diagram of 'Dispersion of Light'.
3. Give scientific reason: Elements belonging to the same group have the same valency.
4. Define: a) Homologous Series b) Alloy.
5. An object takes 5s to reach the ground from a height of 5m on a planet. What is the value of $g$ on the planet?

#### 3 Marks Questions
1. Explain the construction and working of an Electric Motor with a neat diagram.
2. Explain the formation of a rainbow with the help of a diagram.
3. Complete the following table regarding the effects of oxidation:

4. What is Mendel's Periodic Law? Explain the merits of Mendel's periodic table.
5. A convex lens has a focal length of 10 cm. An object is placed at a distance of 20 cm from the lens. Find the position and nature of the image.

## Science and Technology Part 2

### Important Topics
- **Heredity & Evolution**: Darwin's Theory, Evidence of Evolution.
- **Life Processes**: Aerobic/Anaerobic Respiration, Cell Division (Mitosis/Meiosis).
- **Environmental Management**: Ecosystem, Conservation, Disaster Management.
- **Reproduction**: Asexual reproduction in plants/animals, Menstrual Cycle.
- **Social Health**: Stress Management, Cyber Crime.

### Expected Questions

#### 1 Mark Questions (Objective)
1. Find the odd one out: Drying, Salting, Cooking, Soaking with sugar.
2. Complete the analogy: Insulin : Diabetes :: Interleukin : \_\_\_.
3. State whether True or False: Oxidative rancidity is observed in edible oil. (Actually Chemistry, but food related) -> *Correction: "Flowering plants are Gymnosperms" - False.*
4. Name the hormone secreted by the ovary.
5. Identify the symbol: (Symbol of Recycle/Reuse).

#### 2 Marks Questions
1. Give scientific reason: Cell division is one of the important properties of cells and organisms.
2. Distinguish between Aerobic and Anaerobic Respiration.
3. Write a short note on 'Sacred Groves'.
4. Draw a neat labeled diagram of the 'Human Excretory System'.
5. What are the objectives of disaster management?

#### 3 Marks Questions
1. Explain the "Main postulates of Darwin's theory of natural selection".
2. Complete the flow chart regarding 'Process of Energy Production through Aerobic Respiration' (Glycolysis -> ...).
3. Explain the following concepts with examples: a) Genetic Diversity b) Species Diversity c) Ecosystem Diversity.
4. What are the precautions to be taken while using modern technology/internet?
5. Explain the importance of useful microorganisms like Lactobacilli and Rhizobium.

#### 5 Marks Questions
1. Observe the figure of the 'Human Male Reproductive System' and answer the following:
   a) Label the parts A, B, C.
   b) What is the function of Testes?
   c) Which gland contributes to the production of semen?
2. Answer the following questions related to Environmental Conservation:
   a) What is meant by 'Biodiversity'?
   b) Describe any two threatened species.
   c) How can we conserve biodiversity? (Give 3 points).
