# Mathematics Question Bank - Class 10th (Maharashtra Board)

## Algebra (Mathematics Part-I)

### Important Topics
- **Linear Equations**: Cramer's Rule, Graphical Method.
- **Quadratic Equations**: Formula Method, Nature of Roots.
- **Arithmetic Progression**: n-th term ($t_n$), Sum of first n terms ($S_n$).
- **Probability**: Probability of events (Coins, Dice, Cards).
- **Statistics**: Mean (Assumed Mean Method), Pie Diagram.

### Expected Questions

#### 1 Mark Questions (MCQ/Objective)
1. To solve $x + y = 3; 3x - 2y - 4 = 0$ by determinant method, find $D$.
2. What is the value of discriminant for quadratic equation $x^2 + 10x - 7 = 0$?
3. Find the first term and common difference for the A.P.: 127, 137, 147, ...
4. If a die is rolled, what is the probability that the number appearing on upper face is less than 3?
5. Find the class mark of the class 11-15.

#### 2 Marks Questions
1. Solve the following simultaneous equations: $5x + 3y = 9; 2x - 3y = 12$.
2. Find $k$ if $x=3$ is a root of equation $kx^2 - 10x + 3 = 0$.
3. Find the 19th term of the A.P.: 7, 13, 19, 25, ...
4. A card is drawn from a well shuffled pack of 52 playing cards. Find the probability that the card drawn is a spade.
5. Market value of a share is ₹200. If the brokerage rate is 0.3%, find the purchase value of the share.

#### 3 Marks Questions
1. Solve using Cramer's rule: $3x - 4y = 10; 4x + 3y = 5$.
2. Solve the quadratic equation by formula method: $5m^2 - 4m - 2 = 0$.
3. How many three digit natural numbers are divisible by 5?
4. Two dice are rolled simultaneously. Find the probability that the sum of the digits on upper faces is at least 10.
5. Draw a frequency polygon for the given data.

#### 4 Marks Questions (HOTS)
1. A boat travels 16 km upstream and 24 km downstream in 6 hours. The same boat travels 36 km upstream and 48 km downstream in 13 hours. Find the speed of water current and speed of boat in still water.
2. The sum of the squares of two consecutive odd natural numbers is 290. Find the numbers.
3. The sum of the third and seventh terms of an A.P. is 6 and their product is 8. Find the sum of first sixteen terms of the A.P.

## Geometry (Mathematics Part-II)

### Important Topics
- **Similarity**: BPT Theorem, Ratio of areas of similar triangles.
- **Pythagoras Theorem**: Geometric Mean Theorem, Apollonius Theorem.
- **Circle**: Tangent-Secant Theorem, Cyclic Quadrilateral Theorem.
- **Trigonometry**: Trigonometric Identities, Heights and Distances.
- **Mensuration**: Frustum of a cone, Surface Areas.

### Expected Questions

#### 1 Mark Questions (MCQ/Objective)
1. If $\triangle ABC \sim \triangle PQR$ and $AB:PQ = 2:3$, then fill in the blanks: $A(\triangle ABC) : A(\triangle PQR) = \_\_\_$.
2. Out of the dates given, which date constitutes a Pythagorean triplet? (15/08/17)
3. Find the distance between points A(2, 3) and B(4, 1).
4. If $\sin \theta = \frac{7}{25}$, find $\cos \theta$.
5. Find the volume of a cube with side 6 cm.

#### 2 Marks Questions
1. In $\triangle PQR, NM || RQ$. If $PM=15, MQ=10, NR=8$, find $PN$.
2. Find the diagonal of a rectangle whose length is 35 cm and breadth is 12 cm.
3. In the adjoining figure, circle with center O touches the sides of $\angle ACB$ at A and B. If $\angle ACB = 52^\circ$, find measure of $\angle AOB$.
4. Find the coordinates of the midpoint of the segment joining P(0, 6) and Q(12, 20).
5. Draw a tangent to a circle with radius 3.4 cm at any point P on it.

#### 3 Marks Questions
1. Prove that: "The ratio of the intercepts made on a transversal by three parallel lines is equal to the ratio of the corresponding intercepts made on any other transversal by the same parallel lines."
2. Prove that: "Opposite angles of a cyclic quadrilateral are supplementary."
3. Show that points P(2, -2), Q(7, 3), R(11, -1), and S(6, -6) are vertices of a parallelogram.
4. A person standing at a distance of 80m from a church looking at its top makes an angle of elevation of $45^\circ$. Find the height of the church.
5. Construct a triangle $\triangle ABC$ such that $BC = 6$ cm, $\angle B = 45^\circ$, and $\angle A = 100^\circ$. Construct $\triangle A'B'C' \sim \triangle ABC$ where $AB/A'B' = 4/7$.

#### 4 Marks Questions (HOTS)
1. In $\triangle ABC$, the bisector of $\angle A$ intersects side $BC$ at point $D$. A line parallel to side $AC$ passes through point $D$ and intersects side $AB$ at point $E$. Prove that $\frac{AB}{AC} = \frac{BE}{EA}$.
2. A circle with radius 7 cm has two tangents parallel to each other. Calculate the distance between these two tangents. (Application based) - *Correction: This is simple, maybe "Two circles intersect each other..." is more HOTS.*
   *Replacement HOTS*: Two circles touch each other internally. Sum of their areas is $116\pi$ sq. cm and distance between their centers is 6 cm. Find the radii of the circles.
3. A bucket is in the form of a frustum of a cone. The radii of its circular bases are 14 cm and 7 cm. Height is 30 cm. Find the capacity of the bucket.
